FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical turnaround time?

Single commercial spots, UGC hooks, and AI avatars typically see their first polished cut delivered within 48 to 72 hours. Complex multi-scene brand films with custom 3D motion tracking take 4 to 6 business days.

How do revisions and feedback work?

Every production includes 2 structured revision rounds. We share private review links where you can pinpoint timecodes and notes. Revisions are iterated within 24 hours.

Do I own full commercial rights?

Yes, 100%. Upon final payment delivery, all intellectual property, high-resolution master exports, and commercial broadcast rights are transferred to you with zero royalty obligations.

What formats and resolutions are delivered?

Masters are delivered in ProRes or high-bitrate MP4 at 4K UHD (3840x2160) or 1080p, formatted in your required aspect ratios (16:9 widescreen, 9:16 vertical reels, 1:1 square feeds).
